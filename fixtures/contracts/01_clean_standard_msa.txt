MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on October 01, 2026,
by and between Summit Analytics Inc. ("Vendor") and Horizon Global Corp ("Customer").

1. SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES
Vendor agrees to provide data engineering and analytical integration services.

2. COMPENSATION AND INVOICING
Customer shall pay Vendor a total contract sum of $120,000 USD.
Invoices shall be payable within thirty (30) days from invoice date (Net 30).

3. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT EXCEED $300,000 USD.

4. TERM AND TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon sixty (60) days prior written notice.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed under the laws of Delaware.
